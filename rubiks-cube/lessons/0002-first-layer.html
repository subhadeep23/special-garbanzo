Lesson 02 · ~10 minutes · cube in hand

The first layer — by feel, not by rote

You solved a cube following J Perm. Now do the opening stage with the video closed. The first layer is the one part with no algorithm to memorise — it's pure intuition, which makes it the perfect place to prove notation has stuck.

Why this is the confidence win Stages 2–4 are memorised algorithms (later lessons). The first layer is logic — you can always reason your way out. If you can build it unaided, you've removed the biggest chunk of the solve from your "needs the video" list. That's real progress toward the mission: solve unaided.

The goal state

White face fully solved and the side stickers of that whole layer matching their centres — a clean white face with a one-colour "skirt" wrapping each side. Centres never move, so they are your compass: the white centre defines white's home; each side centre defines that side's colour.

Part A — The white cross (do it on the bottom)

Pro habit from J Perm: build the cross on the D (bottom) face, white down. Slower to see, but it sets you up for the whole rest of the solve without re-rotating.

1. Find a white edge

Any piece with white + one other colour. Read its other colour — that names which centre it belongs under.

2. Line it under its centre, then drop it in

Get the edge into the top layer above its target side, with colours matched to the side centre, then turn that face 180° to bring it straight down. Example: blue-white edge above the blue centre → F2 (if front is blue) sends it home.

Petals-up check Done right, the four white edges form a "+" on the bottom AND each side colour runs in a straight line up to its centre. If a side colour zig-zags, that edge is flipped — take it out and re-seat it the other way.

Part B — The white corners

Each white corner has three colours: white + two sides. It lives where those two side colours meet.

1. Park the corner under its slot

Find the corner in the top layer. Spin U until it sits directly below the corner gap it belongs in (between its two matching side centres).

2. The corner cycle — one move, repeat

With the slot at front-right-top and corner waiting at front-right-bottom, repeat:

R U R' U'

...until the white sticker drops in correctly. The same sexy move from Lesson 01 — at most it cycles 3 times. Reset U between attempts if the corner pops to the wrong spot.

Your win today A solved white face + matching skirt, built with the video closed. You just did a third of a full solve from memory. Scramble and rebuild it twice more to lock the intuition in.

Recall check

Cube down, answer from memory.
